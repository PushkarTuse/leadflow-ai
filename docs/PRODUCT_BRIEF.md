# Product and honest demonstration case study

**Working brand:** LeadFlow AI. It has not been checked for trademark/domain availability. **Offer:** a useful website plus a structured enquiry-to-next-step workflow for a small service business. First segment to test: property consultants in India. Position around clearer enquiry handling and owner control, not promised AI revenue.

## The demo business

Greenway Properties is fictional. Aarav, Mira and Dev are synthetic scenarios, not real customers. The demo has no client testimonials, invented company logos, claimed bookings, conversion uplift or revenue figures.

**Scenario:** a visitor wants a two-bedroom apartment in Pune, with a stated budget and preferred conversation time. They enter a short enquiry and explicitly toggle simulated contact permission.

**Before:** an unstructured message that the business owner must re-read and manually organize. **After:** service, budget, urgency and preferred time appear in a mock CRM card, alongside a six-part score and a Telegram-style owner alert. An owner can approve a draft, defer or reject. A draft asks useful questions and proposes a conversation; it never confirms an unavailable property or books a meeting.

**Evidence:** run `/demo` locally. Screenshots in `outputs/screenshots` are captured from the running application. The local dry-run separately demonstrates the agency's internal freelance pipeline; it must not be represented as a customer's prospect data.

**Expected benefit to test:** less time reconstructing enquiries and fewer missing next steps. Measure time spent, response delays, completeness and actual bookings in a consented pilot. No measured improvement exists yet.

## Packages to test

1. **Launch Website**, illustrative starting scope ₹15,000: up to five responsive pages, CTA/intake, basic SEO and owner handover.
2. **Website + Smart Enquiries**, illustrative starting scope ₹25,000: launch website plus structured form, Sheets CRM and owner-approved notifications/drafts. The broader brief's initial range remains ₹15,000–₹30,000 after scope.
3. **Custom Automation**, request quote; projects may be scoped in the ₹20,000–₹60,000 range, with larger API work quoted separately.

Maintenance is an optional, explicitly bounded ₹2,000–₹5,000/month starting discussion. Write down update limits, monitoring tasks, support hours and exclusions. Clients own/pay provider accounts and domains unless an alternative written arrangement is agreed. Free infrastructure is not a 24/7 SLA.

## Pilot acceptance

Agree the business's real intake fields and consent wording; confirm one form submission reaches the right Sheet; confirm one owner alert and manual approval; verify a usable response draft; test mobile; document account ownership and deletion. No production client onboarding should begin before the real form, privacy contact and integration tests are verified.
